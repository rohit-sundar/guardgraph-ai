Two numbers, on purpose. Every malware label in training comes from its family's known ATT&CK techniques, so a split that stratifies on labels puts identical label vectors on both sides of the train/test line — that number measures family recognition, not technique inference. The family-held-out column holds whole families out of each fold, so it can only be scored on families the model never saw. That's the one that means something about a real unseen sample.

Is this app pretending to be a different, legitimate one? Every other tab asks how malicious the code is; this one asks who the app claims to be. A cloned banking or UPI app is fraud whether or not its payload trips a single behavioural anchor, so these findings raise a floor under the verdict rather than adding to the weighted score.
